# Mexican Tomato-Flavored Rice

![Image of Mexican Tomato-Flavored Rice](../img/mexican-tomato-flavored-rice.jpeg)

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 cup long-grain rice
- 2½ cups water
- 1½ Tbsp tomato-flavored bouillon powder (such as Knorr)
- 2 pinches onion powder
- 2 pinches garlic powder

## Steps
1. Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the rice and fry until golden brown, 2 to 3 minutes.
2. Slowly stream in the water while stirring, then stir in the bouillon powder, onion powder and garlic powder.
3. Bring to a boil and cook for 2 minutes.
4. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and cook until the rice has absorbed almost all the liquid, about 15 minutes.
5. Remove from the heat and let sit, covered, until the remaining liquid is absorbed, about 5 minutes.
