# Sausage Egg Roll in a Bowl

![Image of Sausage Egg Roll in a Bowl](../img/sausage-egg-roll-in-a-bowl.jpeg)

## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground pork sausage
- 6 cups coleslaw mix (or shredded cabbage)
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 Tbsp ginger, minced
- 1 Tbsp soy sauce
- ¼ cup chopped green onions
- 1 Tbsp sesame oil

## Steps
1. Heat a large skillet over medium heat. Add the sausage and cook, stirring often to crumble, until cooked through. Do not drain.
2. Add the coleslaw mix, garlic, ginger and soy sauce to the skillet. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes, until the cabbage has softened a bit.
3. Remove from the heat, top with the green onions and drizzle with the sesame oil.
4. Serve immediately.
