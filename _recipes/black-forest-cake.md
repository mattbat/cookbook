# Black Forest Cake

![Image of Black Forest Cake](../img/black-forest-cake.jpeg)

## Ingredients
- Dark Cherry Pie filling (at least 1 can)
- 1 slightly beaten egg
- 1⅔ cups granulated sugar
- 1½ cups milk
- 3 squares (3 oz) unsweetened chocolate, cut up
- 1¾ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ½ cup shortening
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2 eggs
- ½ tsp salt
- 8 oz cream cheese
- 24 oz Cool Whip

## Steps
1. Grease and lightly flour two 9x½" baking rounds.
2. Combine beaten egg, ⅔ cup sugar, ½ cup milk, and chocolate. Cook and stir till mixture just boils; Cool.
3. Combine flour, soda, and salt.
4. Beat shortening 30 seconds. Add remaining 1 cup sugar and vanilla; beat until fluffy.
5. Add eggs one at a time beating 2 minutes after each.
6. Add dry ingredients and remaining 1 cup milk alternately to beaten mixture, beating after each addition.
7. Stir in chocolate mixture.
8. Divide into pans.
9. Bake @350°F for 25-30 minutes. Cool 10 minutes on racks before removing from pans.
10. Beat cream cheese and Cool Whip to make frosting.
11. To assemble layer cake, use frosting to create a ring for containing cherry filling. Add top layer and frost. Refrigerate.

## Notes
- For best results, make the day ahead of time and allow the cake to completely chill in the refrigerator.
